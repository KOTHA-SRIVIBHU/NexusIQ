NexusIQ is an AI-powered enterprise knowledge base platform. It allows teams to upload documents, search across their knowledge base, and get AI-generated answers. The platform supports PDF, DOCX, PPTX, Markdown, and plain text files. Documents are automatically parsed, chunked, and embedded using sentence-transformers. The vector embeddings are stored in a FAISS index for fast similarity search. Users can also create teams, manage members, and set up role-based access control.
